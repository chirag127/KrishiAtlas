# KrishiAtlas — Master Subsidy, Scheme & Public Benefit Register

This register details official Central Government and major State Government agricultural subsidy schemes in India. Every scheme is categorized by its verified operational status, eligibility criteria, documentation, financial ceilings, fund-release mechanisms, and critical operational caveats.

---

## Scheme Status Lifecycle Taxonomy

- **`Active — Open`**: Portal currently accepting applications; funds sanctioned in current budget cycle.
- **`Active — Target Exhausted`**: Scheme is legally active, but district/state budget quotas are saturated; applications queued or paused.
- **`Announced — Pending Guidelines`**: High-level announcement made in budget or press release; operational guidelines (SOP) not yet notified.
- **`Restructured / Merged`**: Scheme absorbed into umbrella programs (e.g., RKVY-RAFTAAR, Krishonnati Yojana).
- **`Discontinued / Closed`**: No new applications accepted; legacy subsidy disbursements only.
- **`Unverified / Ambiguous`**: Official portal down or conflicting state notifications; local verification required at district office.

## Central Government Umbrella Schemes (Centrally Sponsored & Central Sector)

### 1. PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

- **Status:** `Active — Open` (Verified October 2026)
- **Nodal Agency:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW)
- **Portal:** `pmkisan.gov.in`
- **Benefit:** ₹6,000 per year per eligible farmer family, paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 directly via DBT (Aadhaar-seeded bank account).
- **Eligibility:** Landholding farmer families with cultivable landholding in their name (recorded in state land revenue records / RoR / Khatauni).
- **Mandatory Exclusions:**
  1. All institutional landholders.
  2. Farmer families holding constitutional posts.
  3. Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
  4. All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Govt Ministries, Departments, PSEs, autonomous bodies, and regular local body employees (excluding Class IV / Group D).
  5. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more.
  6. All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
  7. Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.
- **Verification Mandatory:** e-KYC (Aadhaar OTP or biometric), Land Seeding (linking Khasra/Khatauni to PM-KISAN ID), and NPCI bank account mapping.
- **Reality Check & Caveats:** Not a wealth-creation mechanism; designed as baseline liquidity support for seasonal inputs. Tenant farmers and sharecroppers without land title are excluded in most states (except state-specific schemes like Odisha's KALIA or Telangana's Rythu Bharosa).

### 2. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

- **Status:** `Active — Open` (Operating window extended through 2032-33)
- **Nodal Agency:** MoA&FW in partnership with NABARD & Scheduled Commercial Banks
- **Portal:** `agriinfra.dac.gov.in`
- **Benefit:**
  - 3% per annum interest subvention on bank loans up to ₹2.00 Crore for a maximum tenure of 7 years.
  - Credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE for loans up to ₹2.00 Crore (fee paid by Govt).
- **Eligible Projects:** Post-harvest management infrastructure (packhouses, sorting/grading units, cold stores, cold chains, ripening chambers, silos, assaying labs) and community farming assets (organic input production, bio-stimulants, smart precision agri-infrastructure).
- **Eligible Entities:** Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), Marketing Cooperative Societies, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), Agri-entrepreneurs, Startups, Central/State agency sponsored PPP projects.
- **Application Process:** Online submission on AIF portal -> Ministry initial vetting -> Forwarded to selected lending bank -> Loan sanction -> Portal logs approval for interest subvention claim.
- **Reality Check & Caveats:** AIF provides an interest discount and credit guarantee, *not* a direct cash grant. The applicant must satisfy commercial bank underwriting, provide margins (usually 10–25%), and demonstrate project cash-flow viability.

### 3. Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

- **Status:** `Active — Target Exhausted / Allocation Based` (State annual quota dependent)
- **Nodal Agency:** National Horticulture Mission (NHM) / National Horticulture Board (NHB)
- **Portal:** `midh.gov.in` / `nhb.gov.in`
- **Benefit:** Capital back-ended subsidy ranging from 35% to 50% of the prescribed cost norm (up to 50% in hilly, tribal, and NE states):
  - Naturally ventilated polyhouse (Type II): Cost norm ₹890–₹1,060/sqm; subsidy 50% (up to 4,000 sqm ceiling).
  - Shade net houses: Cost norm ₹710/sqm; subsidy 50%.
  - High-density fruit orchards (apple, mango, guava, citrus): 40–50% subsidy phased over 3 years.
  - Packhouses, reefer vans, and ripening chambers.
- **Application Procedure:** Prior in-principle approval (IPA) from State Horticulture Department / NHB *before* commencing civil work. Submitting an application *after* constructing the polyhouse or planting disqualifies the applicant from subsidy.
- **Reality Check & Caveats:** Highly competitive; state budgets often exhaust within days of window opening. Unscrupulous polyhouse fabrication vendors frequently inflate quotation rates to capture the subsidy margin. Polyhouse farming without proven local agronomic skills and confirmed commercial B2B off-take has a high commercial failure rate.

### 4. Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

- **Status:** `Active — State Portals Open Periodically`
- **Nodal Agency:** Mechanization & Technology Division, MoA&FW
- **Portal:** `agrimachinery.nic.in` (and state DBT portals like `upagriculture.com`, `dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in`)
- **Benefit:** 40% to 50% subsidy (up to 50% for SC/ST, small/marginal farmers, and women; 40% for other categories) on tractors, laser levellers, happy seeders, rotavators, power tillers, and drone sprayers. Custom Hiring Centre (CHC) establishment grants of up to 40% (up to ₹10–₹25 lakh project cost).
- **Reality Check & Caveats:** Most states use lottery or first-come-first-served token systems. Equipment must be purchased strictly from empanelled dealers and must have an official testing certificate from FMTTI (Farm Machinery Training and Testing Institute).

### 5. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) — Per Drop More Crop (Micro-Irrigation)

- **Status:** `Active — Open`
- **Nodal Agency:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
- **Benefit:** 55% subsidy for Small & Marginal farmers, 45% for Other farmers for Drip and Sprinkler irrigation systems based on standardized unit cost per hectare.
- **Empanelled Vendors:** Direct installation by approved manufacturers (e.g., Jain Irrigation, Netafim, EPC Mahindra).
- **Reality Check & Caveats:** Quotas and vendor price caps vary by state. The farmer pays the net margin, and subsidy is released directly to the vendor post physical field inspection and GPS geotagging.

## The Golden Rules of Agricultural Subsidies

1. **Never build a business plan where viability depends entirely on subsidy receipt.** Treat subsidies as retrospective capital de-risking, not upfront operating margin.
2. **Never initiate expenditure before receiving the official In-Principle Approval (IPA) / Sanction Order.** Construction or procurement prior to sanction invalidates eligibility across nearly all central schemes.
3. **Verify budget availability at the District level (District Agriculture Officer / DHO / KVK).** A centrally approved scheme often sits unfunded in specific districts due to delayed state treasury matching-share releases.
