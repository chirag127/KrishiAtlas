# KrishiAtlas — Methodology, Research Standards & Limitations

This document describes the analytical methodology, data validation protocols, financial modeling frameworks, and inherent limitations of the KrishiAtlas and KrishiGrantha project.

---

## 1. Research & Evidence Grading Hierarchy

Every agricultural, economic, and legal statement in KrishiAtlas is categorized under a strict five-tier evidence grading system:
1. **`high`**: Peer-reviewed scientific field trials published by ICAR/SAU institutes, official statutory gazette notifications, or multi-year cost-of-cultivation data from the Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES).
2. **`moderate`**: Model bankable project profiles from NABARD, standardized university extension manuals, or established agricultural university packages of practices with regional caveats.
3. **`limited`**: Pilot project reports, state-specific case studies with small sample sizes, or emerging agronomic technologies lacking multi-season replication.
4. **`mixed`**: Contradictory empirical outcomes across different agro-ecological zones (e.g., zero-budget natural farming vs. integrated nutrient management yield comparisons).
5. **`not-yet-established`**: Novel commercial claims, unverified vendor guarantees (e.g. exotic superfoods, closed-loop urban agriculture yields) requiring empirical local trial data before commercial capital commitment.

---

## 2. Financial Modeling Assumptions & Limitations

1. **No Guaranteed Returns:** Agricultural profitability is fundamentally contingent upon micro-climate, water reliability, soil microbiome, pest incidence, local labour availability, farm-gate price fluctuations, and post-harvest storage access.
2. **Land Opportunity Cost:** Owned land is never treated as "free." Models incorporate the local cash lease value (Cost B2) to demonstrate whether cultivation generates real economic surplus over passive land rental.
3. **Subsidy Policy Caveat:** Central and State subsidy programs are subject to annual budget ceilings, legislative revisions, and target quota exhaustion. KrishiAtlas financial models evaluate enterprise viability strictly on an unsubsidized baseline.

---

## 3. Legal and Agronomic Safety Boundaries

1. **No Individual Legal Advice:** Land tenure rules, ceiling limits, and tax interpretations provided in KrishiAtlas are for educational and analytical purposes. Specific real estate acquisitions or corporate structures must be reviewed by a practicing revenue advocate and a Chartered Accountant.
2. **Pesticide & Chemical Caution:** Recommendations regarding agro-chemicals are strictly bounded by Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) approved label claims. Off-label pesticide mixtures and unverified veterinary drug usages are strictly prohibited. Readers must observe pre-harvest intervals (PHI) and consult their local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) for localized pest diagnostics.
