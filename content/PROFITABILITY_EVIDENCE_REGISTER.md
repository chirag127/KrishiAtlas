# KrishiAtlas — Master Farm Profitability & Enterprise Evidence Register

This register sets the rigorous financial modeling standards used across all KrishiAtlas economic evaluations. It provides benchmark cost-of-cultivation frameworks, unit economics methodologies, and comparative enterprise returns grounded in empirical Indian data (CACP, DES MoA&FW, NABARD model bankable projects).

---

## 1. The Cost Accounting Standard: CACP Comprehensive Cost Concepts

Agricultural economics in India must distinguish between cash expenditures and full economic costs using the standard cost hierarchy established by the Commission for Agricultural Costs & Prices (CACP):

| Cost Measure | Definition & Included Elements | Practical Farm Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost A1** | All actual paid-out expenses in cash and kind: hired human labour, bullock labour, machine labour (owned + hired), seed, fertilizers, manures, pesticides, irrigation charges, land revenue, depreciation on farm implements, and interest on working capital. | Cash operating expenses incurred out-of-pocket during the crop season. |
| **Cost A2** | Cost A1 + Rent paid for leased-in land. | Actual cash out-of-pocket cost for a tenant farmer who leases land. |
| **Cost A2 + FL** | Cost A2 + Imputed value of unpaid Family Labour (FL). | Minimum baseline used by the Government of India for fixing MSP (at least 1.5x of A2+FL). |
| **Cost B1** | Cost A1 + Interest on value of owned capital assets (excluding land). | Accounts for capital depreciation and asset financing costs. |
| **Cost B2** | Cost B1 + Rental value of owned land (net of land revenue) + Rent paid for leased land. | Full cost of land opportunity and fixed capital assets. |
| **Cost C1** | Cost B1 + Imputed value of family labour. | Total operational and capital cost excluding land rent. |
| **Cost C2** | Cost B2 + Imputed value of family labour. | **The Full Comprehensive Economic Cost.** A crop is truly economically profitable only when gross revenue exceeds Cost C2. |
| **Cost C3** | Cost C2 + 10% of Cost C2 on account of managerial functions performed by the farmer. | Complete enterprise business cost accounting for managerial time. |

## 2. Key Profitability Metrics & Definitions

1. **Gross Revenue (Value of Output):**
   $$\text{Gross Revenue} = (\text{Main Product Yield} \times \text{Farm Gate Price}) + (\text{By-Product Yield} \times \text{By-Product Price})$$
2. **Gross Margin (Operational Cash Margin):**
   $$\text{Gross Margin} = \text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Cost A1 (or A2 for tenant)}$$
   *Note: High gross margin does NOT mean high net wealth, because it ignores land rent, capital depreciation, debt service, and family labour.*
3. **Net Farm Income (Accounting Profit):**
   $$\text{Net Farm Income} = \text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Cost B2 (Cash expenses + Depreciation + Land rent)}$$
4. **Economic Profit (Super-Normal Return):**
   $$\text{Economic Profit} = \text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Cost C2 (includes full opportunity cost of own labour & own land)}$$
   *If Economic Profit is negative, the owner would earn more money by simply leasing out the land to a neighbour and working in a regular job.*
5. **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):**
   $$\text{ROCE} = \frac{\text{Net Farm Income (before interest and taxes)}}{\text{Total Fixed Capital (Land + Borewell + Implements) + Peak Working Capital}} \times 100$$
   *Due to elevated land prices across India, the ROCE on purchased agricultural land typically sits between 1.5% and 4.0% per annum — lower than a bank fixed deposit.*

## 3. Comparative Enterprise Return Profiles (Empirical Benchmarks)

The table below summarizes empirical capital requirements, gestation periods, risks, and typical return profiles across major Indian agricultural enterprises:

| Enterprise Type | Capital Intensity (Capex/Acre) | Gestation to First Cash Receipt | Working Capital / Cycle | Downside Risk Factors | Expected Real ROCE (Excl. Land Appreciation) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Field Crops (Paddy-Wheat / Sugarcane)** | Low (₹10,000–₹25,000 for implements if land is ready) | 4–6 months (cereal); 12–14 months (cane) | ₹25,000–₹45,000 per acre | Canal breach, unseasonal rain, pest outbreak, delayed sugar mill payment. | 2%–5% on owned land; 8%–15% on leased land. |
| **Open-Field High-Value Veggies (Tomato, Chilli, Onion)** | Moderate (₹40,000–₹80,000 for mulching, drip, staking) | 2.5–4 months | ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 per acre | Price crashes (mandi gluts: ₹2/kg realization), virus (LCV), heatwaves. | Highly volatile (-50% to +80% on working capital). |
| **High-Density Fruit Orchard (Guava / Mango / Lemon)** | Moderate to High (₹1.5–₹3.0 lakh/acre for saplings, drip, fencing) | 2–4 years | ₹30,000–₹60,000/acre/yr | Long establishment without revenue, fruit fly, water shortage during fruit set. | 12%–18% post full bearing (Year 5 onwards). |
| **Protected Cultivation (Polyhouse NVPH)** | Very High (₹35–₹45 lakh per acre) | 6–9 months | ₹3.0–₹6.0 lakh per crop cycle | Cladding tears in storms, red spider mite, nematode buildup, power failure, lack of cold-chain buyer. | Negative for 60%+ of novice units; 15%–22% for skilled growers with direct B2B off-take. |
| **Commercial Broiler Poultry (10,000 Bird Contract Farm)** | High (₹30–₹40 lakh shed & automation) | 40–45 days per batch (6 batches/year) | Low under contract (Integrator provides chick & feed; farmer earns growing fee ₹6–₹12/kg) | Heat stroke mortality, avian influenza, high shed depreciation, integrator dispute. | 8%–14% on fixed asset capex. |
| **Commercial Dairy (20 High-Yielding Murrah / HF Cows)** | High (₹25–₹35 lakh for stock, shed, chaff cutter, milking machine) | Immediate (Daily cash flow from Day 1) | ₹80,000–₹1,20,000/month for feed, fodder, AI, veterinary care | Mastitis, repeat breeding, fodder scarcity, calf mortality, milk rejection. | 6%–12% (critically dependent on own green fodder cultivation). |
| **Agri-Service: Custom Hiring Centre (Tractor + Implements)** | High (₹12–₹25 lakh for 50HP tractor, rotavator, laser leveller) | 1 month | ₹40,000/month (diesel, operator wage, maintenance) | Short seasonal window (20–40 days peak per season), bad debts from farmers, machine breakdown. | 14%–22% if annual machine utilization exceeds 800 hours. |
| **Nursery & Seedling Production (Plug Trays)** | Moderate (₹4–₹8 lakh for net-house, germinator, media, trays) | 1–2 months | ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 per batch | Damping-off fungus, unsold perishable seedlings, delayed planting season. | 20%–35% on working capital with established local farmer network. |

## 4. Scenario Analysis Standard: Low / Base / Upside Modeling
All financial crop models in KrishiAtlas must present three explicit scenarios:
1. **Low Scenario (Downside Stress Test):** 20th percentile yield (adverse climate/pest event) + 20th percentile mandi price (harvest glut) + 10% input cost escalation.
2. **Base Scenario (Modal Realistic Outcome):** 5-year average district yield + 3-year average modal farm-gate price + standard CACP input costs.
3. **Upside Scenario (Optimal Management & Premium Realization):** 80th percentile yield + direct institutional/contract buyer premium + zero crop losses.
