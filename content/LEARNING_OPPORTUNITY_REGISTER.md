# KrishiAtlas — Master Agricultural Learning Opportunity Register

This register catalogs free, government-funded, subsidized, and open-access agricultural education, extension training, diploma pathways, entrepreneurship fellowships, and university courses across India.

---

## Opportunity Categories & Funding Taxonomy
1. **`Genuinely Free / Fully Government Funded`**: Zero tuition, zero examination fee, and often includes free accommodation/stipend during residential training.
2. **`Free-to-Audit / MOOC`**: Free access to all lecture videos, readings, and course materials; optional paid certificate fee (₹1,000–₹2,000).
3. **`Subsidized / Sponsored Entrepreneurship Program`**: Course fee covered by Central/State Ministry with bank loan linkage and capital subsidy.
4. **`Fully Funded Fellowship / Degree Scholarship`**: Full tuition waiver plus monthly living stipend for undergraduate, postgraduate, or doctoral study.

## 1. Apex Extension & Farmer Training Institutions (100% Free / Residential)

### Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) — 731+ Districts Across India
- **Nodal Agency:** Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) & State Agricultural Universities.
- **Cost:** **100% Free**.
- **Mode:** In-person, residential / day-training at district KVK farm campuses.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - *Short-duration Farmer Training (1–3 days):* Seed treatment, organic vermicomposting, soil health management, IPM scouting, drone sprayer safety.
  - *Vocational Skill Courses (15–30 days):* Commercial mushroom production, beekeeping (apiculture), commercial dairy management, nursery raising, goat farming, repair and maintenance of farm machinery.
- **Eligibility:** Farmers, rural youth, farm women, aspiring agri-entrepreneurs. No formal degree required; literacy in vernacular language sufficient.
- **Application:** Direct walking-in or telephonic registration at the district Senior Scientist & Head, KVK. Calendar published seasonally before Kharif and Rabi.

---

### MANAGE — National Institute of Agricultural Extension Management, Hyderabad
- **Portal:** `manage.gov.in`
- **Flagship Scheme:** **Agri-Clinics and Agri-Business Centres (AC&ABC) Scheme**
- **Funding:** Supported by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt of India.
- **Cost:** **Free training** (nominal refundable security deposit; residential lodging provided).
- **Duration:** 45 days intensive residential training at designated Nodal Training Institutes (NTIs) across all states.
- **Curriculum:** Agri-business planning, project formulation, market analysis, legal compliance, banking linkage.
- **Post-Training Benefit:**
  - Bank loan eligibility up to ₹20 lakh for individual projects (up to ₹1.00 Crore for group of 5 trained individuals).
  - **Composite Subsidy:** 44% back-ended capital subsidy for Women, SC/ST, and candidates from NE/Hilly states; 36% for General category applicants.
- **Eligibility:** Graduates in Agriculture and Allied subjects (Horticulture, Dairy, Veterinary, Fisheries, Forestry, Agri-Biotechnology) or Diploma holders (with min 50% marks) or Science graduates with PG Diploma in Agriculture.

---

### CCS National Institute of Agricultural Marketing (CCS NIAM), Jaipur
- **Portal:** `ccsniam.gov.in`
- **Focus:** Agricultural marketing, supply chain management, warehousing, and startup incubation.
- **Programs:**
  - **RKVY-RAFTAAR Agri-Business Incubator (R-ABI):**
    - *Pre-Seed Stage (Idea to Prototype):* 2-month training + grant-in-aid up to **₹5.00 Lakh** (Idea stage).
    - *Seed Stage (Early traction):* Grant-in-aid up to **₹25.00 Lakh**.
- **Eligibility:** Any Indian citizen with an innovative agricultural/agri-tech concept.

## 3. Subsidized Skill Certifications: Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI)
- **Portal:** `asci-india.com` (under National Skill Development Corporation — NSDC / PMKVY 4.0)
- **Cost:** **Free under Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)**.
- **Job Roles (National Occupational Standards — NOS):**
  - Micro-Irrigation Technician (AGR/Q1002)
  - Hydroponic Technician (AGR/Q0808)
  - Solar Pump Technician (AGR/Q1103)
  - Tractor Operator & Custom Hiring Manager (AGR/Q1101)
  - Organic Grower / Quality Assayer (AGR/Q1201)
- **Benefit:** Recognized under National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF); enables priority lending under Mudra Yojana and KCC.

## 4. Fully Funded University Scholarships & ICAR Fellowship Pathways

### ICAR National Talent Scholarship (NTS)
- **Agency:** Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).
- **Benefit:** ₹3,000 per month for UG students; ₹5,000 per month for PG students studying in an accredited State Agricultural University (SAU) or Central Agricultural University (CAU) located outside their home state.
- **Selection:** Via All India Entrance Examination for Admission (CUET-ICAR UG / AIEEA-PG).

### ICAR Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) & Senior Research Fellowship (SRF)
- **Benefit:**
  - JRF (Master's): ₹12,640 per month + contingent grant of ₹6,000/year.
  - SRF (PhD): ₹31,000 per month for 1st & 2nd year; ₹35,000 for 3rd year + HRA.
- **Coverage:** 100% tuition waiver in top ICAR institutes (IARI, NDRI, IVRI, CIFE) and SAUs.

## Verification & Enrolment Protocols
1. **Never pay upfront fees to private agents claiming to offer government agricultural certificates.** All legitimate central/state programs are listed on `.gov.in` or `.nic.in` domains.
2. KVK training certificates are free and serve as documentary evidence of technical competence when applying for NABARD/bank loans for dairy, poultry, nursery, or polyhouse enterprises.
3. Review dates for admissions: ICAR CUET entrance windows open annually in February–March; KVK seasonal batches recruit continuously before May (Kharif) and October (Rabi).
