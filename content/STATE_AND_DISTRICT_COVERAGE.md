# KrishiAtlas — State & Union Territory Agricultural Coverage Matrix

This matrix establishes the comprehensive agronomic, institutional, and geographic coverage across all **28 States and 8 Union Territories** of India. Each entry details the official State Agriculture Department portal, digital land records system (Bhulekh), primary agro-climatic zones, staple and commercial crops, predominant soil types, and district-level extension hubs.

## District Extension Coverage Standard
Every KrishiAtlas article referencing regional crop varieties, pest outbreaks, or local economics links directly to the corresponding **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)** district nodal hub. A national directory of all 731 KVKs indexed by state and district is integrated into the site's district search module.
