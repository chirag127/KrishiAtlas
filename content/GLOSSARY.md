# KrishiAtlas — Master Agricultural Glossary

A comprehensive, plain-language, text-to-speech optimized glossary of agronomic, financial, and legal terms used across KrishiAtlas and KrishiGrantha.

---

### A2 Cost
The actual paid-out cash expenses incurred by a farmer during crop cultivation plus the rent paid for any leased-in land. It excludes the economic value of unpaid family labour and the opportunity cost of owned land.

### A2+FL Cost
The A2 paid-out cost plus the imputed economic value of unpaid family labour. This serves as the minimum benchmark used by the Government of India when determining Minimum Support Prices under the Swaminathan Commission recommendations.

### Agri-Clinic and Agri-Business Centre (AC&ABC)
A Central Government scheme implemented by MANAGE that provides free 45-day residential business training and credit-linked capital subsidies of up to forty-four percent for agriculture graduates establishing rural advisory and input enterprises.

### Batai (Sharecropping)
A traditional agricultural tenancy arrangement where the tenant farmer cultivates land owned by another party, with the final harvested produce divided between landowner and tenant according to agreed proportions, traditionally half-and-half.

### Bhumidhar with Transferable Rights
The highest class of landholder under the Uttar Pradesh Revenue Code, possessing permanent, heritable, and transferable ownership rights over agricultural land, subject only to statutory ceiling limits and state land revenue.

### C2 Cost
The comprehensive economic cost of crop cultivation as defined by the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices. It includes all paid-out expenses, rent for leased land, the rental value of owned land, interest on owned fixed capital assets, and the imputed value of family labour.

### Custom Hiring Centre (CHC)
A rural enterprise that rents out modern agricultural machinery such as tractors, laser land levellers, rotavators, and combine harvesters on an hourly or per-acre basis to smallholder farmers who cannot afford to purchase capital equipment.

### Direct Seeded Rice (DSR)
An agronomic technique where un-sprouted or pre-germinated paddy seeds are drilled directly into the main field without raising a separate nursery or puddling the soil under flooded conditions, saving up to thirty percent of irrigation water.

### Farm Gate Price
The net monetary price received by a farmer for their produce at the boundary of their farm or local village collection point before incurring transportation, grading, packaging, or wholesale market commission fees.

### Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
An ecological approach to crop protection that combines biological controls, crop rotation, resistant varieties, and cultural practices, using synthetic chemical pesticides only as a targeted last resort when pest populations exceed economic injury levels.

### Khasra
A legal land revenue record that acts as an annual agricultural field book, recording the plot number, parcel area, soil classification, source of irrigation, cultivated crops, and name of the actual cultivator for every plot in a revenue village.

### Khatauni
The official record of rights in Uttar Pradesh and northern India that registers all landholdings belonging to a landholding family or individual within a village, specifying their legal tenure category and encumbrances.

### Minimum Support Price (MSP)
A guaranteed minimum price floor announced by the Government of India for twenty-three major agricultural crops prior to the sowing season, at which government agencies will procure grain to protect farmers against sharp market gluts.

### Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI)
The mandatory minimum waiting period in days that must elapse between the last application of a registered pesticide molecule and the actual harvesting of the edible crop to ensure chemical residues degrade below safe Maximum Residue Limits.

### Section 10(1) Exemption
The provision under the Indian Income-tax Act of 1961 that exempts agricultural income from Central income tax, subject to the partial integration scheme when non-agricultural income exceeds the basic taxable slab.

### Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
A central government initiative providing subsidies of forty to fifty percent for purchasing modern agricultural machinery, power tillers, drone sprayers, and establishment of village custom hiring centres.

### Theka (Cash Tenancy)
A fixed-rent agricultural lease agreement where the tenant pays an agreed cash sum per acre per year to the landowner, bearing all production risks and input expenses while keeping all harvested produce.
