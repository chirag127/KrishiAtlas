# KrishiAtlas — Master Agricultural Tax Law Register

This register details the taxation of agricultural operations, income, capital gains, GST, and adjacent rural enterprises under the **Income-tax Act, 1961**, relevant **Finance Acts**, Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) circulars, and GST Council notifications.

---

## 1. Direct Taxation: Agricultural Income Under Income-tax Act, 1961

### Statutory Definition: Section 2(1A)
Under Section 2(1A) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, "agricultural income" means:
1. **Clause (a):** Any rent or revenue derived from land situated in India and used for agricultural purposes.
2. **Clause (b):** Any income derived from such land by:
   - (i) Agriculture; or
   - (ii) The performance by a cultivator or receiver of rent-in-kind of any process ordinarily employed by a cultivator or receiver of rent-in-kind to render the produce raised or received by him fit to be taken to market; or
   - (iii) The sale by a cultivator or receiver of rent-in-kind of the produce raised or received by him, in respect of which no process has been performed other than a process of the nature described in paragraph (ii).
3. **Clause (c):** Income attributable to a farm building, subject to strict conditions (the building must be occupied by the cultivator/receiver on or in the immediate vicinity of the land, used as a dwelling house or store-house/out-house, and the land is assessed to land revenue or not situated in specified urban boundaries).

---

### Exemption Mechanism & The Partial Integration Scheme: Section 10(1)
- **Basic Rule:** Under Section 10(1), agricultural income is exempt from Central income tax because Entry 46 of List II (State List) of the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution of India grants exclusive legislative competence over agricultural income taxation to the States.
- **The Partial Integration Scheme (The "Indirect Tax" on Agricultural Income):**
  - Introduced to prevent tax avoidance by non-agricultural taxpayers with large farm incomes.
  - Applies to: Individuals, HUFs, AOPs, BOIs, and Artificial Juridical Persons.
  - **Threshold Test:** Partial integration applies ONLY if BOTH conditions are met:
    1. Net agricultural income exceeds **₹5,000** in the financial year; AND
    2. Non-agricultural income (salary, business profits, capital gains, other sources) exceeds the maximum amount not chargeable to tax (e.g. ₹2,50,000 / ₹3,00,000 in old regime, or basic exemption limit under Section 115BAC new tax regime).
  - **Computation Formula:**
    - *Step 1:* Compute tax on total income = (Non-agricultural income + Net agricultural income) as if it were total taxable income.
    - *Step 2:* Compute tax on (Basic exemption limit + Net agricultural income).
    - *Step 3:* The tax liability = (Tax from Step 1) minus (Tax from Step 2).
    - *Step 4:* Add Health & Education Cess (4%), subtract applicable rebates (e.g. Section 87A).
  - **Practical Impact on Salaried IT Professionals (e.g., TCS Employee with ₹3.6 LPA package):**
    - If non-agricultural salary after standard deduction (₹75,000 in new regime) is ₹2.85 LPA (below the taxable slab under new regime), farm income adds zero tax liability.
    - If non-agricultural taxable income enters higher tax brackets (e.g. 20% or 30%), agricultural income pushes non-agricultural income into higher effective marginal slabs.

### Apportionment Rules: Composite Agricultural & Business Income
When an enterprise both cultivates raw agricultural produce and manufactures finished products, the Income-tax Rules prescribe strict percentage apportionments:

| Rule | Business Activity | Agricultural Income (Exempt u/s 10(1)) | Business Income (Taxable) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rule 7** | General agricultural produce used as raw material in own factory | Market value of raw produce at farm gate is allowed as deduction from factory revenue. | Remaining net profit taxed as business profits. |
| **Rule 7A** | Growing and manufacturing Rubber | **65%** Exempt | **35%** Taxable |
| **Rule 7B(1)** | Growing and manufacturing Coffee (cured) | **75%** Exempt | **25%** Taxable |
| **Rule 7B(1A)**| Growing, curing, roasting, flavouring Coffee | **60%** Exempt | **40%** Taxable |
| **Rule 8** | Growing and manufacturing Tea in India | **60%** Exempt | **40%** Taxable |

### Non-Agricultural Rural Activities (Fully Taxable Under Head "PGBP" or "Other Sources")
The following common rural enterprises are **NOT** exempt under Section 10(1) because they do not involve basic tilling or regeneration from soil:
1. **Poultry farming, Hatchery operations, and Egg production:** Fully taxable business income (Supreme Court in *CIT v. Venkateswara Hatcheries*).
2. **Dairy farming and Milk processing:** Not exempt unless integrated strictly as draft cattle/organic manure on own tilled land; commercial dairy is taxable under Section 28.
3. **Mushroom cultivation on compost trays in dark rooms / vertical racks:** Taxable unless grown directly on tilled open soil (conflicting High Court rulings; safest practice is declaring as taxable PGBP).
4. **Apiculture (Beekeeping) & Honey trading:** Purely commercial business income.
5. **Hydroponics, Aeroponics, and Aquaponics:** Generally treated as commercial industrial horticulture unless substantial basic soil tillage is proven.
6. **Custom hiring centres (farm tractor/machinery rental):** Taxable business income under Section 28.
7. **Storage, warehousing, cold storage, grading, sorting, and packaging for other farmers:** Taxable business services.

## 2. Capital Gains on Agricultural Land: Section 2(14)(iii) & Section 54B

### Classification: Rural vs. Urban Agricultural Land
Only **Capital Assets** attract capital gains tax. Under Section 2(14)(iii), **Rural Agricultural Land** is expressly excluded from the definition of a Capital Asset.

- **Rural Agricultural Land (No Capital Gains Tax):**
  - Land situated in an area outside the jurisdiction of a Municipality/Cantonment Board with a population of 10,000 or more; AND
  - Outside specified aerial distance limits:
    - More than 2 km from a municipality with population > 10,000 but <= 1,00,000.
    - More than 6 km from a municipality with population > 1,00,000 but <= 10,00,000.
    - More than 8 km from a municipality with population > 10,00,000.
  - *Result:* Sale of genuine rural agricultural land yields **ZERO capital gains tax** and need not be reinvested under Section 54B.

- **Urban Agricultural Land (Taxable Capital Asset):**
  - Land situated inside municipal boundaries or within the 2/6/8 km aerial radii.
  - *Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG):* Held <= 24 months; taxed at applicable slab rates.
  - *Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG):* Held > 24 months; taxed under Section 112 (12.5% without indexation post Finance (No. 2) Act 2024).

### Relief Under Section 54B: Transfer of Land Used for Agricultural Purposes
- **Eligibility:** Individual or HUF.
- **Condition:** Land must have been used by the taxpayer or their parents for agricultural purposes for at least **2 years** immediately preceding the transfer date.
- **Relief:** Exemption up to the amount reinvested in purchasing any other agricultural land (rural or urban) within **2 years** from the transfer date, or deposited in Capital Gains Account Scheme (CGAS) before filing ITR u/s 139(1).

## 3. Goods and Services Tax (GST) Framework for Agriculture

### Fresh Agricultural Produce: 0% GST (Exempt)
- Unprocessed cereals (paddy, wheat, maize), pulses, oilseeds, fresh vegetables, fresh fruits, unmanufactured tobacco leaves, green tea leaves, raw sugarcane.
- Fresh milk, unpasteurized curd, natural honey (not packaged/labelled under trademark).

### Intermediate Farm Operations & Services: Exempt Under Notification No. 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate)
- Cultivation, harvesting, threshing, plant protection, pruning, packaging for primary market.
- Renting or leasing of agro-machinery without operator.
- Loading, unloading, packing, storage or warehousing of agricultural produce.
- Agricultural extension services.

### Taxable Inputs & Machinery (GST Rates)
- **Fertilizers:** Chemical fertilizers generally taxed at **5% GST**.
- **Pesticides, Insecticides, Fungicides:** Taxed at **18% GST**.
- **Tractors:** Taxed at **12% GST** (engines and certain parts at 18%).
- **Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Systems:** Taxed at **12% GST**.
- **Food Processing Machinery:** Taxed at **18% GST**.

## 4. Mandatory ITR Filing and Documentation Standards
1. **ITR-1 (Sahaj):** Can ONLY be used if agricultural income is up to **₹5,000**.
2. **ITR-2:** Mandatory if agricultural income exceeds **₹5,000** and individual has no business income (e.g. salaried employee earning farm income). Requires completing Schedule EI (Exempt Income) specifying:
   - Gross agricultural receipts.
   - Expenditure incurred on agriculture.
   - Unabsorbed agricultural loss of previous 8 assessment years.
   - Pin code, district, and area measurement of land.
   - Ownership status (Owned / Leased).
   - Irrigation source (Canal / Tube well / Rainfed).
3. **ITR-3 / ITR-5:** Mandatory when running integrated commercial enterprises (seeds, nursery, poultry, custom hiring, dairy).
4. **Audit Requirement Under Section 142(2A):** High agricultural receipts (> ₹10 lakh) without documentary evidence (land records, mandi purchase slips / J-Forms, canal water receipts, electricity bills) frequently trigger scrutiny notices (CASS) requiring full substantiation.
