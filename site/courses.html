How to Acquire Practical Agricultural Skills at Zero Cost

You do not need a paid private coaching institute or expensive overseas consultancy to master agricultural science in India. The Government of India, ICAR, and State Agricultural Universities provide world-class, fully funded residential workshops and digital coursework.
