Master Agricultural Subsidy & Benefit Register

Verified operational status, DBT procedures, mandatory exclusions, and fund-exhaustion caveats for major Central and State agricultural programs.

The Golden Rules of Farm Subsidies in India

1. Never initiate capital expenditure before receiving the official In-Principle Approval (IPA) / Sanction Order. Procuring machinery or erecting structures before sanction invalidates eligibility across nearly all central schemes.
2. Never build a farm business model where financial solvency depends entirely on subsidy receipt. Treat subsidies as retrospective capital de-risking.
3. Verify budget quotas at the district level. State treasury matching shares often delay fund releases even when central portals appear active.

Protected Cultivation & Orchards
 Centrally Sponsored
midh.gov.in 		35% to 50% Capital Subsidy
Naturally ventilated polyhouses (₹890–₹1,060/sqm cost norm); high-density orchards; packhouses. 		Eligible: Individual farmers, FPOs, cooperatives.
