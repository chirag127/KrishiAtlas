Section 2(1A) Definition

Agricultural income requires basic soil operations (tilling, sowing, planting). Rent derived from agricultural land (Theka) is 100% agricultural income. However, commercial poultry, dairy, hydroponics, and machinery hiring are classified as taxable business income under Section 28.

Capital Gains & Land Transfer

Sale of Rural Agricultural Land (outside municipal limits and specified 2/6/8 km aerial boundaries) attracts ZERO capital gains tax because it is excluded from Section 2(14)(iii). Urban agricultural land transfers can claim capital gains exemption under Section 54B if reinvested in agricultural land within 2 years.

GST on Farm Operations

Fresh unprocessed crops (cereals, fruits, vegetables, green tea, raw sugarcane) are taxed at 0% GST (Exempt). Intermediate agricultural services (cultivation, harvesting, threshing, storage) are also 100% exempt under Notification No. 12/2017-Central Tax. Fertilizers carry 5% GST; tractors and drip irrigation systems carry 12% GST.
